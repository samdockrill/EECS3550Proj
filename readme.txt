Nutriweek is a meal planning and nutrition tracking program by Group 17

To begin, either tap an existing week's icon or create a new week.
The version submitted comes pre-loaded with one example week full of random foods.
Once in a week view, you can see your meal plan at a glance. Tap any day for a more detailed view.
Once in a day view, you can see the total nutrition value for all the foods planned for that day
These are displayed as a ratio X / Y, where Y is the total recommended daily value from the FDC (or the limit, where applicable).
You can add as many foods as you'd like to a day by clicking the "Add food button".
In the add food menu, you can pick one of multiple options to add a food to the day.
The simplest is the "TapnType" option, where it will list autocomplete suggestions from its database as you type the food name.
The second option will open up a barcode scanner view, letting you instantly add any product by scanning it.
Finally, you can manually input food data for custom items or things not in the database. They will be permanently added to it!
At any time, you can hit 'go back' to return to the previous screen.
